Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 · Meru Art Studio

Welcome to Meru Art Studio ("we", "us", "our", or "the Studio"). We operate the digital art commission booking platform located at https://meru-art-studio.vercel.app. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through our application in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), general international privacy frameworks, and Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our service, we collect the minimum necessary data to manage character art commissions:

  • Google Authentication Data: When you choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive your basic profile information provided by Google OAuth (specifically: your email address, display name, and unique Google account identifier). We do not request access to Google Drive, contacts, private files, or passwords.
  • Commission Brief Details: Information you voluntarily submit in your commission request, including character names, visual descriptions, reference sheets, preferred start dates, and communication notes.
  • Payment Proof: Screenshots of GCash or PayPal transaction receipts uploaded by clients to reconcile deposits and final commission payments.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate studio business operations:

  • To verify your identity and associate your commission briefs with your client account.
  • To track and display your order progress (from brief review, sketching, coloring, to deliverable handoff) in your personalized order room.
  • To send you realtime notifications when art milestones (WIP updates, sketch sign-offs) are ready for your review.
  • To prevent duplicate spam requests and uphold availability limits on our studio calendar.

3. Google User Data Policy Compliance

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Meru Art Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We strictly do NOT use Google user data for serving advertisements, and we do NOT transfer or sell Google user data to any data brokers, information resellers, or third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored within an encrypted Supabase database with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Only you (as the verified account owner) and the artist administrator have permission to view your commission briefs, chat logs, and uploaded files. Reference images and deliverables are hosted in private storage buckets accessed solely through short-lived signed URLs.

5. Data Retention and Erasure

You have the right to access, review, export, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. Upon request or following commission completion and audit periods, you may request that your uploaded reference materials and personal identifiers be permanently purged from our database and storage systems.
